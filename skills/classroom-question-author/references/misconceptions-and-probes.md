# Misconceptions and Probes

For each expected wrong answer record a stable ID, objective ID, lesson-language
diagnosis, teaching message, nudge, minimal probe topology, and exact visible
result expected to fail.

Probe designs must differ from the passing design by the misconception itself.
Do not sabotage hidden instance, service-time, edge, or trait fields. Run every
probe and require it to trigger its own message; overlapping messages need an
explicit priority and a test.
